add destination, source increases the destination. sub destination, source subtracts the source from it. Neither changes a register used only as the source. Small signed results in this section fit in 64 bits.

mov rax, 17
add rax, 8
sub rax, 4
The running value is 17, then 25, then 21.

Keep a running total in the destination; operand order matters for sub.
