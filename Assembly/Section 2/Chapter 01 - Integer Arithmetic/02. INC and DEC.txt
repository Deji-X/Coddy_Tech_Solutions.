INC and DEC



inc adds one and dec subtracts one. They are useful for counters and inclusive endpoints.
Unlike add and sub, they leave the carry flag unchanged;
other arithmetic status flags can change.

mov rax, 6
inc rax
dec rax
inc rax
Two increments and one decrement change 6 to 7.

Use inc or dec for a change of exactly one.
