The two-operand form imul destination,
source multiplies signed integers and keeps the result in the destination.
We use small products that fit in 64 bits. This form does not put a second result in rdx.

mov rax, -4
mov rbx, 6
imul rax, rbx
A negative number times a positive number is negative: -4 * 6 = -24.

For two-operand imul, the destination receives the product.

Use these instruction excerpts inside the supplied solve routine;
the challenge provides the input and output code.
