Unsigned Division



div divisor divides the unsigned 128-bit value in rdx:rax.
For our nonnegative 64-bit inputs, set rdx to zero first.
The quotient goes into rax and the remainder into rdx.
The divisor must be nonzero, and the quotient must fit.

mov rax, 23
mov rdx, 0
mov rbx, 5
div rbx
Dividing 23 by 5 gives quotient 4 in rax and remainder 3 in rdx.

Before unsigned division of a value in rax, clear rdx and use a nonzero register divisor.
