Signed branches interpret the same bits as signed integers.
After cmp a, b, use jl for a < b, jg for a > b, jle for a <= b, or jge for a >= b.
They account for both sign and overflow flags.

mov rax, 0
mov rbx, -3
cmp rbx, 2
jge .done
mov rax, 1
.done:

Signed -3 is less than 2, so the jge branch is not taken.

Use jl, jg, jle, or jge for signed order comparisons.
