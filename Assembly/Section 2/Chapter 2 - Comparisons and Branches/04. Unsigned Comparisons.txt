Unsigned branches interpret every bit as part of a nonnegative magnitude.
After cmp a, b, jb means below, ja means above, jbe means below or equal,
and jae means above or equal.
These differ from signed branches when the highest bit is set.

mov rax, 0
mov rbx, 8
cmp rbx, 5
jbe .done
mov rax, 1
.done:
Unsigned 8 is above 5, so the result becomes 1.

Choose the branch family to match the signed or unsigned meaning of the data.
